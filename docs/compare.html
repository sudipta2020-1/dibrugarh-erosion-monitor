This page compares the proposed method, SWiFT-Bank, with two baseline methods on the same images and the same monitoring windows. The two baselines use the same rule (a pixel is water if it was water on at least half of its clear dates) at 20 m and at 10 m, and have no statistical test of change. All figures are for the river corridor, which is land within 1 km of the river channels.

How the three methods differ

SWiFT-Bank proposed

  1. Every clear Sentinel-2 date is read at 10 m. The SWIR band is sharpened from 20 m to 10 m with the NIR band.
  2. On each date, the share of water in each pixel (0 to 1) is found by unmixing between local pure-water and pure-land spectra.
  3. The change in mean water share between the two windows is tested for each pixel with Welch's t-test, with the false discovery rate held at 5 %.
  4. Lost land is the sum of the water-share change times the pixel area. Patches are kept if they lose at least 0.02 ha and their combined p-value is below 0.01.

Baseline 10 m baseline

  1. The same dates are read at 10 m, with the same sharpened SWIR band.
  2. Each date is classified as water or land with an edge-based Otsu threshold on MNDWI.
  3. A pixel is water if it was water on at least half of its clear dates.
  4. Water and land labels of the two windows are compared, and patches of at least 5 pixels (0.05 ha) are kept. There is no test of change.

Baseline 20 m baseline

  1. The same dates are read at 20 m.
  2. Each date is classified as water or land with an edge-based Otsu threshold. Radar is used where optical dates are missing.
  3. A pixel is water if it was water on at least half of its clear dates.
  4. Patches of at least 10 pixels (0.4 ha) are kept. There is no test of change.

Null test. The current window is split into odd and even dates, and each method is run on the two halves. No erosion can happen between them, so any change found is a false alarm. This measures how often a method reports change that is not there.

Limits. The three methods are compared here with each other and with independent labelled data from other places. The accuracy of the smallest losses in this district still has to be checked in the field.
