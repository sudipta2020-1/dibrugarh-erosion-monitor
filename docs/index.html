Method in brief

Each run builds cloud-free Sentinel-2 composites and Sentinel-1 radar composites for the most recent monitoring window and for the same calendar dates in the baseline year. Comparing the same months limits false change caused by seasonal river level and crop cycles. Water is mapped from the MNDWI index, with radar backscatter used where clouds hide the optical image. Land that turned into water is reported as bank erosion, and water that turned into land as accretion. Annual soil loss is estimated with RUSLE (A = R × K × LS × C × P), where the cover factor C is derived from NDVI and the slope factor LS from the Copernicus 30 m DEM. Patches are ranked by type and area to give a field verification list.

Rainfall erosivity (R) and soil erodibility (K) currently use uniform district values, so soil loss should be read as relative risk. All results need field confirmation before conservation works are planned.

Data: Copernicus Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (via Microsoft Planetary Computer), district boundary from geoBoundaries. Updated automatically every month.
